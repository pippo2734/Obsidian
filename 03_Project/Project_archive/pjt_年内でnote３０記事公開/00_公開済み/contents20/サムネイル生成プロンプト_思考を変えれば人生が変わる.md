# サムネイル生成プロンプト

Create a stylish YouTube/Note thumbnail in a clean and minimal lifestyle design.
The theme is: 「マインドセット転換・思考法」

### Composition:
- Bright white or light beige desk background with natural soft lighting.
- Items related to the theme on the desk:
  - **Books as main props** (stacked books showing 4 books, or an open book with bookmark)
  - Coffee cup on the desk
  - Notebook or journal with a pen
  - Reading glasses (optional)
- A human hand or partial gesture (like gently holding a book or pointing at text) can appear for warmth.
- Add elegant Japanese text in the center.

### Text layout:
- Top small heading: 「厳選」
- Middle large bold title: 「思考を変える本」
- Bottom small subtext: 「4冊で人生が変わる」

### Typography & style:
- Clean, modern Japanese fonts (similar to MUJI or minimal magazines)
- White, beige, and light gray color palette
- Natural and calming atmosphere, slightly blurred background
- No heavy contrast, no clutter
- Aspect ratio: 16:9
